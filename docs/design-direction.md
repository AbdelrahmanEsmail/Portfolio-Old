# Design direction

The portfolio keeps its own charcoal, ivory, lime, and brass identity. Game references are carried by the visual language around the work, rather than by logos, character art, slogans, or copied interfaces.

- **Urban case files:** numbered records, compact metadata, and framed product evidence give the projects a dossier rhythm.
- **Frontier field notes:** warm brass annotations, understated serif accents, and chapter markers bring a journal quality to the page.
- **Exploration:** original contour paths and connected diamond waypoints suggest a route through the work. The field guide records sections encountered during the visit without blocking any content.

Primary navigation and calls to action stay plain. Actual screenshots remain unchanged. Experiments are labeled, and projects without verified media get readable notes rather than fabricated interfaces. The IDE and pixel companions retain the more playful details in localized areas.

All added identity graphics are inline SVG or CSS. The field guide needs no storage, account, sound, reward claim, or required interaction. Motion follows the existing reduced-motion behavior.
